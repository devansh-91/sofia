Har line ke saare versions suno aur 1–5 do (5 = bilkul insaan jaisa, 1 = robot). Naam chhupe hain taaki bias na ho, par har letter poore page pe ek hi awaaz hai (A har line pe wahi A). Sab rate karne ke baad Naam dikhao dabao. Saari lines rate karna zaroori nahi — 4–5 lines bhi kaafi hain.

Result

RTF = bolne mein laga time ÷ audio ki length (1 se kam = real-time se tez). VRAM = engine ne GPU pe kitna liya (Sofia ka Qwen load ho toh ~1 GB hi bachta hai — "Sofia ke saath" column yahi test hai; "CPU only" wale GPU chhoote hi nahi). CER = Whisper ne jo suna wo text se kitna alag (kam = saaf).
